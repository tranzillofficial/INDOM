## Choreography
I starts at 0 seconds, translates from +140px to its resting position. N starts at 0.38s, D at 0.76s, O at 1.14s and M at 1.52s. Later letters translate from -55px and fade in; the web version adds a left-to-right clip reveal. Signature fades in at 2.2s. Replay is available. Reduced-motion users get the complete static mark.
Figma motion tracks were verified structurally. The remote video download is blocked by the environment; no MP4 artifact is claimed. MagicPath build completed and its stored preview shows the early IN phase. Final web resting state is included in the desktop and mobile screenshots.
